Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to FlowSnap (流光), an Android app with package name com.lightpick.photos.play, developed by starone studio. It explains how information is handled and how you can contact us.

Local use and media access

FlowSnap is an app for local photos and videos. It does not require an account or sign-in and does not display ads. Its core media features run on your device. The app does not offer a feature for uploading your photos or videos to developer-operated servers. The current version does not automatically transmit your photos, videos, embedded location information or local diagnostic records off your device, or share them with third parties. Exports, sharing and email contact that you initiate are separate.

To use relevant features, you may grant access to photos and videos or select media through a system picker. Access depends on the permissions you grant and your system settings. You can change or revoke permissions in your device settings at any time; features that depend on those permissions may then be unavailable.

Photos, videos and associated information

Media you allow the app to access may contain capture times, file information and location metadata already embedded in a photo or video. This information is processed with the media on your device; this does not mean the app is obtaining your device’s live location. Before sharing or exporting media, consider the content and metadata that the files may contain.

Local diagnostic information

The app may save operational logs and diagnostic files locally on your device (such as app startup and error records) to help troubleshoot problems. If you choose to send diagnostic information by email, it will be handled as described in the email contact section below.

Billing component and third-party services

The current version includes the Google Play Billing component, but in-app purchases have not been enabled. The app does not ask you to provide payment information to the developer. Google Play’s own handling of information is governed by Google’s applicable policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can control media access through your device’s permission settings. You can use your device’s storage management options to clear app data or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may not delete original or exported media stored in your system photo library or shared storage. To delete those files, use your photo library or file manager. Check whether you need a backup before deleting files.

Contact by email

If you email us for support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to address your inquiry and any necessary follow-up. We retain this information only as long as needed to address your inquiry, provide necessary follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you provided by contacting the email address in this policy. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Email service providers may process email information under their own policies.

Policy updates

If app features or information-handling practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Where changes are significant, we will provide notice through an appropriate channel.

Contact

For questions about this policy or how information is handled, contact the developer at the email address below.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026-10-21

适用范围

本隐私政策适用于 FlowSnap（流光），Android 应用包名为 com.lightpick.photos.play，开发者为 starone studio。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息，以及您可以如何联系我们。

本地使用与媒体访问

FlowSnap 是用于本地照片和视频的应用，无需注册或登录，也不展示广告。本应用的核心媒体功能在您的设备上运行，不提供将照片或视频上传到开发者服务器的功能。当前版本不会自动将您的照片、视频、媒体附带的位置信息或本地诊断记录传出设备，也不会由本应用将其分享给第三方。您主动导出、分享媒体或通过邮件联系我们的情况另行处理。

使用相关功能时，本应用可能需要您授予访问照片和视频的权限，或由您通过系统选择器选择媒体。访问范围取决于您授予的权限及系统设置。您可以随时在系统设置中更改或撤销权限；撤销后，依赖这些权限的功能可能无法使用。

照片、视频与附带信息

处理您允许访问的媒体时，相关文件可能包含拍摄时间、文件信息，以及照片或视频本身附带的地理位置元数据。此类信息随媒体在设备上处理，不代表本应用正在获取设备的实时位置。请在向他人分享或导出媒体前留意文件可能包含的内容及元数据。

本地诊断信息

本应用可能在设备上保存本地运行记录和诊断文件（例如应用启动及异常记录），用于排查问题。如您自行选择通过邮件提供诊断信息，这些信息将按下文的邮件联系说明处理。

内购组件与第三方服务

当前版本集成了 Google Play Billing 内购组件，但尚未启用内购功能。本应用不要求您向开发者提供付款信息。Google Play 自身对信息的处理受 Google 的相关政策约束。

您的选择与删除

您可以通过系统权限设置控制本应用对媒体的访问。您可以使用设备提供的存储管理功能清除本应用数据或卸载本应用。清除应用数据或卸载应用不一定会删除保存在系统相册或共享存储空间中的原始或导出媒体；如需删除这些文件，请在相册或文件管理器中操作。删除前请确认是否需要备份。

通过邮件联系我们

如果您发送电子邮件寻求支持，我们会收到您主动提供的邮箱地址、邮件内容及附件，并将这些信息用于处理您的咨询及必要的后续沟通。我们仅在处理咨询、必要的后续沟通及履行适用法律义务所需的时间内保留这些信息。您可以通过本政策列出的邮箱申请删除您提供的信息。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。邮件服务提供商可能会按照其自身政策处理邮件信息。
